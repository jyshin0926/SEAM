Speech-conditioned affective music generation

Given a spoken utterance and an emotion-free instrument prompt, generate background music whose valence–arousal follows the speech — without putting mood words in the text.

IEMOCAP Session 5 · piano, violin string, and drum · frozen SA3 medium

Paper companion

Task 1 · emotion alignment

Play the speech, then the three music clips. All systems share the same SA3 backbone. Ours uses speech tokens and an emotion-free prompt; the others stay text-only.
