SEAM · ICASSP 2027

Speech-conditioned affective music generation

Given a spoken utterance and an emotion-free instrument prompt, generate background music whose valence–arousal follows the speech — without putting mood words in the text.

Frozen SA3 medium wav2vec2-ER tokens IEMOCAP Session 5 Prompt: piano, violin string, and drum
Abstract Task 1 · alignment Task 2 · intensity

Abstract

Listen to the speech, then the music

Text-to-music models usually take affect from a word such as angry. SEAM instead injects a short stream of speech-derived valence / arousal tokens into a frozen Stable Audio 3 DiT. The text prompt stays instrument-only; intensity is a residual in SER space against a matched baseline clip.

This page is the paper companion. Task 1 asks whether the generated music matches the emotion of the reference speech. All systems share the same SA3 backbone and instrument bed.

Task 1

Emotion alignment

Two IEMOCAP Session 5 clips per emotion. Play the speech first, then compare an instrument-only SA3 anchor, SA3 lexical, and SA3 VA against Ours.

No speech tokens. Official IEMOCAP valence and arousal in text.
